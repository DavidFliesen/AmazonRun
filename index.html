Amazon Run

Mission: Pilot a rescue boat down the Amazon, gather supply crates, and reach the jungle extraction point.

This version starts calmly. The first stretch teaches you what objects mean before the river gets dangerous.

Collect: Yellow medical supply crates. They add fuel, repair a little hull damage, and move the mission forward.
Avoid: Gray rocks, brown logs, green crocodiles, and red raider boats. These damage your hull.
Control: A/D or ←/→ steer. W/S or ↑/↓ shift your boat up or downriver. Space boosts, but uses fuel.
Goal: Collect 8 crates and survive to extraction. Early river = relaxed. Deep jungle = challenge.
