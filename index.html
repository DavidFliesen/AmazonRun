Amazon Run

Mission: cruise the Amazon, deliver 3 supply crates, and reach the river outpost.

This is now a slower, more scenic river adventure. The goal is to explore, read the river, and enjoy the jungle instead of getting smashed every few seconds.

Yellow crates: supply deliveries. Collect only 3 total. Each one repairs your boat and adds fuel.
River hazards: rocks, logs, sandbars, and crocodiles appear gradually. Most are avoidable with gentle steering.
Controls: A/D or ←/→ steer. W/S or ↑/↓ move forward/back in the view. Space gives a short boost.
Vibe: golden light, jungle banks, ruins, birds, slow water, and a third-person boat view.

Press Enter or tap to begin.
